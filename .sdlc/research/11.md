# Research: E8 Portfolio review and sell discipline (ST-8.1 to ST-8.7)
**Goal**: swapnil-agrim/nivesh#11 · **Lane**: large (recommend 4 ordered slices, ST-8.5 to 8.7 deferred) · **Date**: 2026-10-11

## Goal in one paragraph
Make every holding justified by a stored thesis and give the owner a Hold/Add/Trim/Exit/Review list with tax impact. ST-8.1: `nivesh thesis onboard` drafts a horizon, a why-own of at most 60 words and 2-4 measurable kill criteria for each holding without a thesis (from FA/TA/news views), asks accept/edit/skip, and stores an accepted Thesis with created date and review date (default 90 days). ST-8.2: a HoldingReview agent (versioned prompt, read-only tools, schema-validated, SDK faked) rates each kill criterion met/not met/unknown with evidence, while every section 15.6 trigger is computed in code; a met kill criterion can never end as HOLD without an explicit override reason. ST-8.3: an India tax-lot engine (per-lot holding period, STCG/LTCG, days to LTCG, unrealised gain, tax now vs at LTCG, cheapest-tax lots for a TRIM, FIFO noted) from owner-maintained config. ST-8.4: rebalance moves when drift exceeds a band (default +-5pp) using new cash, then ST-8.2 trims, then lowest-tax lots, with pro-forma and turnover % within the limit. ST-8.5 to 8.7 depend on unbuilt stories and are delivered only where dependencies exist, otherwise explicitly deferred. Sources: PID ST-8.1..8.7 (lines 1132-1200), FR-29 (249), FR-39 (264), sec 13 Thesis (372), sec 14.1 reviewer (399), sec 15.4 tax (524), sec 15.6 rules (538-547), OBJ-5/OBJ-9 (78, 82), acceptance `.sdlc/acceptance/11.md`, design `.sdlc/design/1.md`. Checked against worktree `.sdlc/work/11` (HEAD baf8383 = E1..E7 merged, E7 shipped via #82). Constraints: package `nivesh`; claude-agent-sdk pinned and faked in unit tests; deterministic engines in Decimal; no write/order tools for agents; the commit secret-scanner refuses credential-looking identifiers/literals.

North-star: drop-in project; no `.sdlc/context/north-star.md` was consulted (none required by the brief).

## Decisions the plan must make (with recommendations)
- **D-1 Thesis model.** `Thesis{thesis_id, security_id, created_at, horizon (positional_1_6m|long_term_1y_plus, same labels as `CommitteeVerdict.horizon`), why (<=60 words, enforced by the existing `_words` helper pattern), kill_criteria (2-4 items), review_date (default created + `review.default_review_days`=90), status, source ('onboarding'|'decision')}`; `KillCriterion{id, text, metric (optional engine field e.g. `roce_pct`, `price_vs_sma200_pct`), comparator (lt|gt|lte|gte), threshold (Decimal str), unit}`.
