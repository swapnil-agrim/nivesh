### Owner decisions resolved here (the dossier left them open; safest option chosen, recorded in ADR-0010, owner sign-off issue in Deferred X9)
- **OD-1 Tax config home and precedence (TD-1).** New `IndiaTax` model hung on the existing `TaxSettings` as `tax.india` with fields `long_term_days`, `short_rate_pct`, `long_rate_pct`, `ltcg_exemption_inr`, **all `None` by default** (no rate, day count, exemption, cess or surcharge in code). Precedence is per asset, never merged: Indian direct equity/ETF lots use `tax.india`; mutual-fund lots use the existing `mf.tax` (`MfTax`, the fund doctor's source; no exemption field, so none is applied); US lots use `tax.us_long_term_days` for the long-term flag only and **tax is always `None` with reason "US tax not modelled"**. `Profile.tax_rates` stays in the file, is **never read**, and is documented as legacy in the ADR and in a comment in `config/profile.yaml` (no field removal: removing it is a profile contract break). Unset rate means gain and days only with a reason (same as `mf_lots.tax_impact`). Every tax figure printed carries the label "estimate from your config; not tax advice". Losses are not set off against gains (tax per lot is on `max(gain, 0)`): this overstates rather than understates. The exemption, when set, is applied **once per scenario** (one holding's sale or one TRIM selection) with the stated assumption "no other long-term gains realised this year".
- **OD-2 HoldingReview vocabulary.** `schema_version` bumps to `2`; `action` becomes upper-case `HOLD | ADD | TRIM | EXIT | REVIEW` (matching the AC and the verdict ladder style). v1 is replaced, not kept beside it (no producer or consumer exists: verified by `rg -n "HoldingReview" nivesh_* ` showing only `schemas.py` and the generated JSON). Contract note in ADR-0010.
- **OD-3 India-equity lot source = report unavailable.** Indian direct equity has no dated lots (`analysis_data.py:490-493`, CAS `Txn` rows are MF only, InvestRight rows carry only `avg_cost`). The engine returns `reason="holding period unavailable: no dated lots"` for those holdings, never a guessed date or an `avg_cost`-derived lot. No lot CSV importer is built (Deferred X4).
- **OD-4 Turnover cap is per run.** `review.turnover_limit_pct` (default 30, from OBJ-9) caps the proposed turnover of **one rebalance proposal**; annual (year-to-date) turnover needs the ledger (ST-12.1) and is deferred (X5). The output says "per-proposal cap; annual turnover not tracked yet".
- **OD-5 Scope of holdings**: thesis and HoldingReview cover `asset_class` equity and etf (IN and US); mutual funds are out (fund doctor, #59/#65) and the CLI says so. Rebalancing covers every holding with a `value_inr` (MF included, since `target_allocation` is by asset class); rows without `value_inr` (US with FX unavailable) are excluded with a note.
- **OD-6 Reviewer data access**: `holding_review` allow-list = `mcp__engine__fa_compute, ta_compute, valuation_range, red_flags` (no holdings-derived tool, no `mcp__holdings__*`); `thesis_draft` has **no tools and no servers** (debate-style: it drafts only from the analyst views it is given). Weights, lots, tax and trigger results are code-computed JSON in the prompt.
- **OD-7 Override semantics (safest reading of "never HOLD without an explicit override reason")**: an agent-supplied `override_reason` can never produce HOLD or ADD when a kill criterion is met or rule 1/4 is triggered; it can only lift TRIM to REVIEW, and the reason is recorded in `overrides`. HOLD over a met criterion requires an owner decision, which belongs to `/decide` (ST-8.6, deferred X2). The AC fixture (met criterion, no override) therefore yields TRIM or EXIT.
- **OD-8 Thesis history**: append-only rows with `status in active|superseded|closed` and a unique partial index allowing one `active` row per security; this goal writes only `active` rows for holdings that have none (no supersede path is built; `source in onboarding|decision`, only `onboarding` is written).

### Design decisions fixed here (from dossier D-1..D-11, with corrections)
- **D-1 Thesis model** (`nivesh_core/thesis.py`, pydantic, `extra="forbid", strict=True`): `KillCriterion{criterion_id:int>=1, text (1..40 words), metric:str|None, comparator: lt|lte|gt|gte|None, threshold: Decimal|None, unit:str=""}`; the triple `metric/comparator/threshold` is all-or-none. `Thesis{thesis_id:int|None, security_id:int, created_at:date, horizon: positional_1_6m|long_term_1y_plus, why (<= 60 words), kill_criteria (2..4, unique ids, **at least one machine-checkable**), review_date:date, status, source}`. Word and count limits are PID content rules, kept as module constants (`WHY_MAX_WORDS = 60`, `MIN_CRITERIA = 2`, `MAX_CRITERIA = 4`), not config. `review_date` default = `created_at + review.default_review_days` (90), computed by a function taking both dates (no clock). `KillCriterion` is reused by `ThesisDraft` (single definition).
- **D-2 Schemas** (pydantic single source, regenerated JSON, drift test): `ThesisDraft{schema_version=1, security_id, as_of, horizon, why, kill_criteria[2..4], evidence: list[Ref] (min 1, `ViewRef` into the analyst views given, checked with `debate.pointer_errors`), data_gaps[]}`. `HoldingReview v2{schema_version=2, security_id, as_of, action, confidence: low|medium|high, reasons: list[Reason{code, text}] (min 1), criteria: list[CriterionCheck{criterion_id, status: met|not_met|unknown, evidence: list[Evidence]}] (met/not_met need >= 1 evidence), triggers: list[TriggerResult{code, status, detail}] (code-filled), thesis_status: intact|weakened|broken|unknown, valuation_stretch: bool|None, tax_note: str (code-filled), override_reason: str = "", overrides: list[str] (code-filled), evidence: list[Evidence]}`. Field names avoid every redaction part (`key`, `account`, `client`, ...) and every write word.
- **D-3 Tax engine** `nivesh_engine/tax_lots.py`: `TaxLot(acquired_on, quantity, cost_per_unit|None, source)`, `TaxRule(long_term_days|None, short_rate_pct|None, long_rate_pct|None, exemption_inr|None)`; `tax_lots(lots, price, as_of, rule) -> LotTaxReport` with per lot `holding_days, long_term (bool|None), days_to_long_term (int|None, 0 when long-term), gain, tax_now, tax_at_long_term (same price assumption, labelled), saving` and totals plus `reason`; `cheapest_lots(lots, quantity, price, as_of, rule, *, fifo: bool) -> LotPick{picks[(acquired_on, quantity)], tax|None, gain, fifo_note|None, reason|None}`: ranking is loss lots first, then lowest estimated tax per unit, then oldest; with an unset rate it ranks by long-term first then gain per unit and returns `tax=None` with the reason; with `fifo=True` the picks are oldest-first and `fifo_note` states the tax difference versus the unconstrained ranking (informational wording only, never "the depository requires"). Reuses `returns.holding_days/is_long_term`.
- **D-4 Sell rules in code** `nivesh_engine/review_rules.py` (module named without the word "sell": the AST write-word scan forbids it in names and docstrings): `criterion_status(criterion, value) -> met|not_met|unknown`; `sessions_below_sma(closes, window) -> int|None`; `declining_quarters(series, n) -> bool|None`; `evaluate_triggers(facts, cfg, *, horizon) -> tuple[TriggerResult, ...]` for the six PID 15.6 rules with codes `kill_criterion, fundamental_deterioration, valuation_stretch, concentration, below_sma200_weak_rs, better_use_of_capital` and statuses `triggered|clear|not_evaluable|not_applicable`. Missing input is `not_evaluable` with a reason, **never clear**. Rule 2 compares each of the last `deterioration_quarters` quarters with the previous one for both revenue growth and operating margin (detail says "vs prior quarter; no plan figures are stored"). Rule 3 = own-history percentile of the configured multiple over `valuation_history_years` > `valuation_percentile_min` and revisions direction <= 0. Rule 4 = position weight > `Profile.max_position_pct` or sector weight > `Profile.max_sector_pct` (Decimal via `Decimal(str(x))`, the existing helper pattern). Rule 5 = `not_applicable` unless horizon is positional; triggered when `sessions_below_sma(closes, 200) >= below_sma200_sessions` and the RS change is < 0. Rule 6 = always `not_evaluable: no idea run (ST-9.4)`. `action_floor(proposed, criteria, triggers, *, override_reason, review_due) -> (action, overrides)` with ladder `ADD > HOLD > REVIEW > TRIM > EXIT` (code only lowers): a met criterion or triggered rule 1/4 caps at TRIM (or REVIEW when `override_reason` is non-empty, OD-7); triggered rules 2/3/5 or `review_due` cap at REVIEW; otherwise the proposal stands. Machine-metric criteria are judged by code; when code has a value it wins (disagreement logged in `overrides`); when code's value is unknown, an agent `met` stands and an agent `not_met` becomes `unknown` (cautious merge).
- **D-5 Rebalance** `nivesh_engine/rebalance.py`: `propose_moves(classes, holdings, *, band_pp, new_cash, turnover_limit_pct) -> RebalancePlan{moves, pro_forma, turnover_pct, within_band, notes}` at asset-class level (TD-8). Order fixed by the AC: (1) new cash to under-weight classes in proportion to their shortfall; (2) reductions in over-weight classes from holdings flagged EXIT (whole value) then TRIM (up to the class excess); (3) then other holdings of the class by lowest `tax_per_inr` (unknown tax last, noted). Proceeds go to under-weight classes. Each class is moved only to its **band edge** (minimum turnover). Turnover = sum of reductions / pre-proposal portfolio value x 100; the proposal stops at the cap (partial last move) and notes what stays out of band. Proposal objects only; names avoid write words (`propose_moves`, `Move(kind: "add"|"reduce")`).
