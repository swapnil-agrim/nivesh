## Step 0: spike results (done; everything below was observed, not assumed)

### Transport and auth
- Endpoint `POST https://mcp.tapetide.com/mcp`, JSON-RPC 2.0, `{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"tools/call","params":{"name":<tool>,"arguments":{...}}}`, headers (an Authorization header using the Bearer scheme with the token), `Content-Type: application/json`, `Accept: application/json, text/event-stream`, `User-Agent: nivesh/0.1`.
- `tools/call` works WITHOUT a prior `initialize` and without `mcp-session-id`. Response was `application/json` (not SSE) for every call; keep an SSE fallback parser (last `data:` line) because the Accept header allows it and the server may change.
- Body shape: `{"result":{"content":[{"type":"text","text":"<JSON string>"}]},"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1}`; the inner `text` is a JSON string, parse it a second time. Failures are `result.isError == true` with free text in `content[0].text` (still HTTP 200).
- Tools used: `get_financials(symbol, section?, periods?)`, `get_shareholding(symbol, type="quarterly")`. `symbol` is the plain NSE symbol (RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, TCS, INFY, ITC all resolved). `tools/list` is open (55 tools, read from the research note's capture).
- Errors observed:
  - missing credential gives HTTP 401 `{"error":"authentication_required","error_description":"token required"}` plus a WWW-Authenticate header (Bearer scheme, resource_metadata);
  - invalid credential gives HTTP 401 `{"error":"invalid_token","error_description":"Invalid or expired token"}`. (No 403 was seen; treat 401 and 403 the same.)
  - unknown symbol: HTTP 200, `isError:true`, text `Error: Not found (tapetide-api): {"error":"Company not found: ZZNOSUCH. ...`;
  - bad section: HTTP 200, `isError:true`, text `Error: get_financials: unknown section "bogus". Valid values: profit_loss, quarterly, balance_sheet, cash_flow, ratios ...`;
  - rate limit / quota (not triggered, from the vendor README): HTTP 200 with `isError:true` and a "Rate limit exceeded" text, `Retry-After` header on denials. Daily window resets on IST midnight. Classifier must therefore key on `isError` text AND a `Retry-After` header, and any unrecognised `isError` text still degrades to a reason, never to zero rows.
- Response headers carry only `x-ratelimit-reset: 1791916200` (= 2026-10-13 18:30:00 UTC = 00:00 IST, the daily reset). There is NO `X-RateLimit-Remaining` or `Limit` on responses (CORS exposes them, the server did not send them), so remaining quota cannot be read; the client must count its own calls.
- Quota used by the spike: 23 successful authenticated calls (1 all-sections, 20 sections, shareholding, pledge); 4 error probes (2 with the real token, 2 unauthenticated or fake token) are not billed per the vendor ("only successful calls count"). About 27 free calls remain until the reset at 18:30 UTC today.

### Data shape (identical for all 5 names; Screener-style rows)
- Per section object: `{section, period_type, periods (oldest first), row_order, data {row: {period: number|null}}, availability [{period, available_from "YYYY-MM-DD", basis "reported"|"estimated"}], percent_rows?, pct_changes, scraped_at}`. Period labels are `"Mar 2026"` (month name + year, fiscal period end month), annual sections also carry the pseudo-period `"TTM"` (must be skipped).
- One section per call returns every period held. The all-sections call (no `section`) is CAPPED to the latest 5 periods per section even with `periods=40` (`meta.note` says so), so depth needs one call per section. All-sections adds a sixth section `top_ratios` (period_type `snapshot`, `data {"Book Value","Face Value","ROCE","ROE"}`), observed for RELIANCE: Face Value 10, Book Value 668.
- Units: INR crore, plain numbers (int or float), including INFY (INR, NOT USD; FY26 sales 178,650 equals the filing). EPS is rupees per share. Percent rows (`OPM %`, `Tax %`, `Financing Margin %`, `Dividend Payout %`) are rounded integers: do not use.
- Basis: consolidated (RELIANCE and HDFCBANK numbers include subsidiaries, see accuracy). Standalone is not offered. `Net Profit` is profit after tax INCLUDING minority interest (non-controlling), while `EPS in Rs` is per share to the parent: RELIANCE FY26 Net Profit 95,754 vs attributable 80,775 (EPS 59.69 x 1,353.2 crore shares = 80,775).
- Depth (observed, all five names identical): `quarterly` 13 quarters (Jun 2023 to Jun 2026), `profit_loss` 12 fiscal years Mar 2015 to Mar 2026 plus TTM, `balance_sheet` and `cash_flow` 12 fiscal years. Meets ST-4.4 (>= 5 annual, >= 12 quarters). `ratios` section (debtor/inventory/payable days, ROCE) is not needed and is not fetched.
- Rows per section:
  - `quarterly` and `profit_loss` (non-bank): `Sales, Expenses, Operating Profit, OPM %, Other Income, Interest, Depreciation, Profit before tax, Tax %, Net Profit, EPS in Rs` (+ `Dividend Payout %` annual only). `Operating Profit` = Sales - Expenses, before depreciation, excluding other income (an EBITDA-style figure).
  - banks (HDFCBANK): `Revenue` instead of `Sales` (total income), `Financing Profit`, `Financing Margin %` (meaningless, negative) instead of `Operating Profit`/`OPM %`; `Interest`, `Depreciation`, `Profit before tax`, `Net Profit`, `EPS in Rs` present. Balance sheet has `Borrowing` (singular) and `Deposits` (non-banks have `Borrowings`, no `Deposits`).
  - `balance_sheet`: `Equity Capital, Reserves, Borrowings|Borrowing, [Deposits], Other Liabilities, Total Liabilities, Fixed Assets, CWIP, Investments, Other Assets, Total Assets`. No cash, receivables, inventory, current assets or liabilities.
  - `cash_flow`: `Cash from Operating Activity, Cash from Investing Activity, Cash from Financing Activity, Net Cash Flow, Free Cash Flow, CFO/OP`. No capex row; capex = CFO - Free Cash Flow (derive).
- No bank asset-quality rows (GNPA, NNPA, NIM, CASA, CAR): those metrics stay "unavailable" with their existing reason; nothing is stored as zero.
- Point-in-time date: `availability[].available_from`, `basis: reported` = real declaration date, `estimated` = regulatory-lag guess (e.g. 2024-05-31 for FY24). Observed split: quarterly 8 reported (since Sep 2024) + 5 estimated; annual only the latest 2-3 periods reported, the other ~10 estimated. Values are latest figures (restatements included); only the timing is original. So history before ~Sep 2024 has an approximate `filed_at`.
- Shareholding: `get_shareholding(symbol, type="quarterly")` returns `data[0].data {Promoters, FIIs, DIIs, Government, Public, Others, No. of Shareholders}` by quarter label (23 quarters, Dec 2015 and Mar 2021 to Jun 2026), NO filing date, plus a top-level `promoter_pledge {as_of_date, pct_of_promoter_holding, pct_of_total_shares, status}` for the LATEST quarter only (RELIANCE: 0 pledged, 2026-06-30). One call gives promoter %, public % and latest pledge, so `get_promoter_pledge` (2 quarters only) is not needed. Caveat: its own `promoter_holding_pct` (51.32) differs from the `Promoters` row (50.48) for the same quarter; use only the `Promoters` row.

## Design decisions (record in ADR-0015)
- D1 Primary source: `nivesh_adapters/fundamentals_tapetide.py` `TapetideFundamentals(Adapter)`, `name="fundamentals_tapetide"`, `source="tapetide"`. Keyed on the NSE symbol `sec.symbol` for India securities (exchange NSE or any symbol Tapetide accepts); no `bse_code` needed. BSE `IndiaFundamentals` stays untouched and is used only if Tapetide produced neither annual nor quarterly rows AND a `bse_code` alias exists.
- D2 Seam: `ingest_fundamentals(..., tapetide: Callable[[], TapetideFundamentals] = TapetideFundamentals)` next to the existing `india=` seam; `nivesh_cli/market.py` gets `_tapetide()` like `_india_fundamentals()`.
- D3 Network: small POST JSON-RPC helper inside the new adapter (`http_get` unchanged), client from `make_client(self.name)`, token fetched per call with `get_secret("TAPETIDE_TOKEN")`, never stored on the instance, never in params/cache/logs/exception text. Pacing: `RateLimiter` from `nivesh_adapters/edgar.py` at 1 call per 1.1 s (vendor burst limit 60/min; no config change).
- D4 Degradation (always "unavailable with reason", never a crash or zero): missing token -> `SourceUnavailable("TAPETIDE_TOKEN not set; create a free token at tapetide.com/settings/tokens and run `nivesh secrets set TAPETIDE_TOKEN`")`; HTTP 401/403 -> `SourceUnavailable("Tapetide rejected the token (HTTP 401): regenerate it ... ")` (no retry; retrying could burn quota); `isError` with Retry-After or rate-limit text -> `RateLimited("Tapetide daily quota reached (free plan: 50 calls/day, resets 00:00 IST); cached data used if any")`; "Company not found" -> `SourceUnavailable("Tapetide has no financial statements for SYM")`; any other `isError` -> `SourceUnavailable` with the first 160 chars of the sanitised text; HTTP 429/5xx -> existing mapping; malformed JSON/missing keys -> `DataQualityError`. `Run.get` already turns these into `failed source ...` lines and `cached_fetch` already serves stale cache on failure.
- D5 Mapping to the stored vocabulary (all values in absolute INR = crore x 10^7 as `Decimal`, currency `INR`; EPS as given):
  | stored item | Tapetide row | note |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | revenue | `Sales` or `Revenue` (bank) | |
  | net_income | `Net Profit` | includes minority interest (disclosed, see D8) |
  | eps | `EPS in Rs` | |
  | pretax_income | `Profit before tax` | |
  | income_tax | `Profit before tax` - `Net Profit` | derived, only if both exist |
  | interest_expense | `Interest` | |
  | depreciation_amortization | `Depreciation` | |
  | total_equity | `Equity Capital` + `Reserves` | annual only |
  | total_debt | `Borrowings` or `Borrowing` | annual only |
  | total_assets | `Total Assets` | annual only |
  | deposits | `Deposits` | new item, banks only; used to classify the company as financial |
  | cfo | `Cash from Operating Activity` | annual only |
  | capex | `Cash from Operating Activity` - `Free Cash Flow` | derived, positive outflow, annual only |
  | shares_out | (`Equity Capital` x 10^7) / `Face Value` | latest annual period only, from the snapshot call; skipped with a reason when Face Value is absent |
  NOT stored: `operating_income` (the existing India convention is a profit-before-tax proxy and `fa`/`valuation` deliberately mark EBIT/EBITDA metrics unavailable for India; Tapetide's `Operating Profit` is an EBITDA-style figure and mapping it would need an fa/valuation change; follow-up), `cash`, receivables, payables, inventory, current_*, dividends_per_share, gross_profit, bank asset-quality items. `TTM` columns and null cells are skipped.
- D6 Point-in-time: `filed_at` = `available_from`. Rows whose basis is `estimated` are written with source `tapetide_est`, rows with `reported` with source `tapetide` (two `write_fundamentals` calls; PK already includes `source` and `filed_at`; no migration). The report counts both so approximate history is visible; ADR states history before about Sep 2024 is approximate. A period with no `availability` entry is skipped and listed in `skipped` (no invented date).
- D7 Shareholding: `get_shareholding(quarterly)` -> `ShareholdingRow(period_end, filed_at = period_end + 21 days (SEBI shareholding filing deadline; approximate, source `tapetide_est`), promoter_pct, public_pct, promoter_pledged_pct = latest `promoter_pledge.pct_of_promoter_holding` for its `as_of_date` quarter only, else None)`. Failure here does not fail the financials.
- D8 Honest coverage: `FundamentalsReport` gains `notes: list[str]` and `estimated_rows: int`; the CLI prints them. Notes emitted: (a) when latest annual `net_income` differs from `eps x shares_out` by more than 3%, "net income includes minority interest (Tapetide Net Profit); EPS-based figures are attributable" (RELIANCE triggers); (b) "N of M rows carry an estimated filing date"; (c) any section that failed. `fa` stays unchanged for this except D9.
- D9 fa unlocks, minimal and test-first: (i) `sector_kind_of` also treats a `deposits` row as financial (HDFCBANK has no sector in the master, so bank rows are otherwise treated as a general company); (ii) remove the blanket `_INDIA_GAPS` override so `cfo_to_pat`, `fcf_margin_pct`, `accruals_ratio_pct` are computed from the rows when present and otherwise say "no <item> filed ..." (BSE-fallback rows still lack those items, so the honest reason is unchanged in effect). `_EBIT_BASED` India override stays. Growth, net margin, ROE, debt/equity then compute from Tapetide rows without other fa changes (`_book` anchors on revenue/net_income; `_cagr` needs 4 annual points; `_roe` needs 2 equity points).
- D10 Cache/quota: reuse `cached_fetch` (7-day `fundamentals` TTL, stale-on-failure). Params are `{"resource": "financials", "symbol": S, "section": <one of quarterly|profit_loss|balance_sheet|cash_flow|snapshot>}` and `{"resource": "shareholding", "symbol": S}`; the cache holds the parsed inner JSON, never headers or token. One section failing does not block the others; no automatic retry anywhere.
