## Risks and mitigations
- Starter facts unverified (400/day, 5,000/month, monthly anchor, per-minute limit, failed-call billing, header presence). Mitigation: conservative counting (every attempt counts, never refunded), `month_start_day` and plan numbers in config, `quota set` overrides, the ledger records outcomes so the owner can compare with the dashboard.
- Shared quota: other Tapetide clients spend the same allowance and the vendor reports no remaining count. Mitigation: `quota set --used-today N [--on DATE]` manual entries; documented in ADR-0016 and the runbook.
- Attributable profit is approximate (weighted EPS against period-end shares) and exists for the latest fiscal year only; India names without `eps` or `shares_out` (BSE-only rows, a missing face value) keep ROE and net margin on the consolidated figure, labelled `consolidated_minority_unknown` with the reason (orchestrator decision after plan drafting: flag rather than drop `fa` coverage). - Many call sites for the source-aware read (about a dozen signatures). Mitigation: the optional-argument design keeps old behaviour, and the AST completeness test fails on any forgotten reader.
- Merge friction on `fundamentals_tapetide.py`, `market_ingest.py`, `nivesh_cli/main.py` across Tasks 0 to 7. Mitigation: strict sequencing, each test file extended by one appended block.
